🎯 How to Play

📌 Goal

Find all the correct answer words from the word list!

💡 Hints System

Tap hints to get clues about the words:

Animal
✓ 4 Correct ✗ 1 Wrong

→ 4 are correct answers

🎮 Two Modes

✓ Select Correct

Tap words you think are correct answers

⚠️ Wrong tap = lose 1 heart

✗ Mark Wrong

Mark words you think are wrong (no penalty)

💡 Marked words can't be selected

❤️ Hearts & Stars

• You have 3 hearts - lose all = game over

• Use fewer hints to get more stars ⭐
